### Project Description

In this project, you will be required to reference the playbook you created in previous activities. You must create a minimum of three policy outlines. Your policy outlines should state:

1. What the overall purpose of the policy is  
2. Why this policy is important from a security point of view  
3. What activities must be performed, how frequently they need to be performed, and by whom  
4. The related playbook and what it will be used for within the policy  
5. Consequences of non-compliance for both an individual and the company

## Instructions

Instruction

Create your outlines, clearly referencing the Playbook you have developed and providing a link for the evaluators to access it.

Your policy should not include any procedural steps; only high-level objectives and goals that must be completed. Your playbook should include step-by-step instructions on how objectives should be accomplished.

For example:

In a log monitoring policy, you may say something like, “All logs should be reviewed weekly for any signs of malicious activity.”

In your log analysis playbook, you would have details on how the analysis is performed. What tools should be used, what commands should be run, and what activity should you be looking for in the logs?

A policy document is more high-level (objective-focused), while a playbook is more like a step-by-step workflow.

Note

Why? Policies are often rated Green in TLP. Procedures are often YELLOW as they may contain sensitive data or instruction. Playbooks should be RED, as they may expose critical information to bad actors.

Instruction

When your policy set is complete, create a slideshow that announces your Policies to your Organization.

Communication is an important part of any Policy that affects people, they must be informed and educated on the Policy. Ensure that your slideshow explains “Why” the policy is being created, and the consequences of non compliance to both the company and the individual.

When done, submit your slideshow along with your policy document for grading.

## Playbook criteria

Your playbook should include the following:

1. Actionable steps to accomplish specific goals  
2. Step-by-step instructions that can be followed in a sequential manner to accomplish the goal  
3. Each step should be assigned to a specific group/department  
4. Command line or application commands that should be run (if applicable)  
5. Location of applicable files on a given system  
6. All four of the NIST incident response process steps  
7. It must contain a procedures section that shows specifically under what conditions your playbook will be called (a flowchart may be a good to illustrate this)
