### Project Description

In this project you will create a custom workflow that involves specific individuals as required to solve an incident. You will be introduced formally to the concept of a client playbook. You will create a sample template for any communication in the scenario.

## Instructions

1. Select an incident type \[phishing, ransomware, data breach, etc\] to help you identify workflows and escalations within the blue team for the scenario below  
2. Explain who should be involved in the incident response workflow and describe what triggered their involvement. Give specific examples of the questions you would ask and why the answer affected the workflow.  
3. Read the Scenario carefully and extract as much information as you can. Identify the information needed to create your incident response workflow and what information you need to gather from the client to formulate your workflow.  
4. Warning  
5. In this scenario the client has asked that both them and a third-party provider be contacted in the case of a suspected breach. The client does not have in-house capabilities to handle the breach and therefore asks for only an executive summary, with the full report and actionable items going to the third party.

   ## The Cat and Box Scenario

Box is a small manufacturing company that specializes in making cardboard boxes for all sizes of cats. Cat is a consultant that works for a managed security service provider (MSSP) and is contracted by Box to look after their security needs.

Mr. Percy F., CEO of Box, has contracted an SOC to monitor their network, including the network itself, systems, and data. Percy, being a smart businessman knows that he knows nothing of systems, but should still be aware of anything that might impact his business so he has asked that Miss Misha F., his shift and production manager, be informed of any major highlights and any potential impact that might be caused by any breach of the system.

Percy has further instructed the SOC to provide Cat with all detailed information that might be needed to stop or remediate an attack since Cat is overseeing all of their security needs. As part of this, all playbooks and workflows developed for Box are to go directly to Cat for approval and she will take care of passing them along to any and all parties as necessary. You have been brought in as a specialist to aid with developing appropriate playbooks and workflows for Box and Cat.

  ## Project Outcomes

In completing this project, learners will be able to create a professional looking client playbook with your incident response workflow and sample communication letters/templates.

#### Warning

You will be encountering new terms in this project. \* Playbook: A documented set of procedures for responding to cybersecurity incidents. \* SOP (Standard Operating Procedure): Standardized instructions for routine operations in cybersecurity. \* Client Playbook: Tailored guidelines for clients to detect, prevent, and respond to cybersecurity threats.
